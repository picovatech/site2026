The challenge

Many shoppers in Bangladesh hesitate to buy online because of unclear pricing, unfamiliar sellers and payment worries. The goal was a store that feels simple and safe from the first click.

What we built

One storefront for five everyday categories, a cart that updates live, and cash on delivery at checkout so nobody has to pay before they receive. Order tracking and customer accounts keep shoppers informed after they buy.

Project Overview

Picobazar is an e-commerce marketplace built by Picova Tech, created to give everyday shoppers in Bangladesh a simple, trustworthy way to browse and order online. It brings together several everyday shopping categories — fashion, gadgets, beauty, kids' essentials and grocery — into a single, honestly-tagged storefront, with cash on delivery and doorstep shipping designed to reduce the friction that keeps people from shopping online.

Key Features

  • Multi-category storefront (Fashion, Gadgets, Beauty, Kids, Grocery)
  • Shopping cart with live cart drawer
  • Wishlist
  • Customer login & account area
  • Order tracking page
  • Cash on delivery checkout
  • Category mega-menu navigation
  • Email newsletter sign-up
